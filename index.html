What We Do

Our Services

AC Repair & Summer Tune-Ups

Refrigerant checks, coil cleaning, capacitor and contactor fixes, and tune-ups so your home keeps up with Lubbock heat.

Heating & Furnace Repair

Furnace startups, heat-pump defrost issues, ignition and safety checks before the next Panhandle cold front.

System Replacement & New Install

Load-aware equipment matching, proper line set and electrical connections, and startup testing on new heat pumps and straight-cool systems.

Preventive Maintenance Plans

Seasonal visits to clean drains, tighten electrical connections, and catch worn parts before they fail on the hottest or coldest day.

Ductwork & Airflow

Sealing leaky plenums, improving return air, and balancing supply so every room gets airflow without overworking the blower.

Indoor Air Quality & Thermostats

Media filters, humidification options, and Wi-Fi thermostat setup so you control comfort and dust from one place.

Emergency HVAC Service

After-hours and weekend support when the system shuts down and you need someone in Lubbock who answers the phone.

Light Commercial HVAC

Package units, split systems, and tenant spaces for retail, offices, and small shops in and around Lubbock.
